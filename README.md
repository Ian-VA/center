### Centr: The first data center pollution & permitting analytics platform

<img width="3186" height="1984" alt="Screenshot_20260426_035201" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/56557634-bb5f-43c5-9f0e-de3907ea912c" />

Center is the first data center pollution & permitting analytics platform to help datacenters get permitted early and avoid regulation delays while informing communities and developers of health costs and pollution. 

Users create a hypothetical data center, add its generators, and simulate its pollution impact and health cost in real time. An AI model calculates its permitting timeline and probability using a US-wide dataset of data centers. 

The COBRA model (EPA-endorsed) is used for pollution calculations.

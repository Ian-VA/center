The project, titled Rockdale Technology Park 1, is located in Conyers, about 20 miles southeast of Downtown Atlanta. The estimated 53-acre site would have 837,500 square feet for data center space in three buildings, according to a filing with the state's Developments of Regional Impact program. The developer, SDP Acquisitions LLC, is an affiliate of Strategic Real Estate Partners, an Atlanta-based industrial real estate company. It is requesting to rezone the land and plans to complete the project in 2028. The company could not be reached for comment.

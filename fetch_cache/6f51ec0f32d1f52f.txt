Pete , (Munster) Times of Northwest Indiana Sunday, September 21, 2025 12:35 PM A $11 billion data center campus, the largest private sector investment in state history, is now under construction outside New Carlisle just across from the LaPorte County line. And now a developer is seeking to build a $12 billion data center nearby that would eclipse the ongoing Amazon Web Services project as the largest in Indiana history. But this time, the plan has drawn public opposition and received an unfavorable recommendation from the St. Joseph County Plan Commission last week. Mall-O-Manor LLC wants to build a data center campus on 1,000 acres of land in unincorporated St. Joseph County. "Only about 300 acres would have development with the rest remaining ag land and buffering," St. Joseph County Economic Development Executive Director Bill Schalliol said. "I believe it would be one of the largest projects in the history of the state. Previously, the AWS project at $11 billion was the largest single investment." That project, which will allow Amazon to provide cloud computing services for business clients, is expected to employ about 1,000 workers. Michigan City has been pursuing more housing projects in part due to the expected demand it will create. Mall-O-Manor expects to create 300 full-time jobs at its proposed site. It would build 14 buildings on the sprawling campus. "The square footage has not been finalized," Schalliol said. "It would service the tech space." The end user has not been disclosed yet. It's expected to be one large user, pointing to a tech giant. "The project, if approved, will provide another major regional development in the Indiana Enterprise Center that will provide the region with more jobs, more investment, and more tax base," Schalliol said. "These projects have a benefit to the community during the construction process but also provide high-skill jobs for the region." The Plan Commission voted against the project because it was inconsistent with the comprehensive land use plan and due to public opposition to the project.

Stack files to build two more data centers outside Atlanta, Georgia - DCD Home News The Data Center Construction Channel Stack files to build two more data centers outside Atlanta, Georgia Company to add more facilities to the two previously planned November 12, 2024 By Dan Swinhoe Have your say Stack Infrastructure has filed to build two more data centers outside Atlanta, Georgia. Stack submitted a Developments of Regional Impact filing with the Georgia Department of Community Affairs to expand its upcoming campus in Douglas County's Lithia Springs. Stack's ATL02 site – Stack Infrastructure The filing is for two additional two-story data centers at its ATL02 Lithia Springs campus; one 196,030 sq ft (18,210 sqm) facility, and another 227,260 sq ft (21,115 sqm) facility. The company first filed to develop its Lithia Springs campus in October 2023. The project was set to see two three-story data centers developed at 808 Factory Shoals Road. Both buildings will span 146,380 sq ft (13,600 sqm). The project completion date is listed as May 2026 and valued at $380.9 million. On its website, Stack said the Lithia Springs site spans 39 acres and offers 48MW of capacity. It has been listed as a built-to-suit opportunity since at least 2021. The property is currently greenfield land. Stack’s first Atlanta facility was one of the eight original facilities seeded to the company by IPI in 2019. Previously operated by T5, ATL01A offers 7MW across 102,500 sq ft (9,520 sqm). An expansion announced in 2020 could add a second, 12MW, building. Atlanta is becoming a major data center hub. The likes of Digital Realty, CoreSite, Switch, Google, Microsoft, Flexential, H5, QTS, and numerous others have or are developing data centers in the Atlanta area – largely around Lithia Springs or Douglasville.

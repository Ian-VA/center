Rural Jasper County data center proposal sparks controversy | KSNF/KODE | FourStatesHomepage.com JASPER COUNTY, Mo. — A proposal to build a data park in eastern Jasper County is sparking controversy. Geronimo Power out of Minnesota is developing a project that would pair a data center between Sarcoxie and LaRussell with a solar farm just north of that. The two would sit near the Liberty Energy Park near Fir Road and County Road 30. Several residents living in the area are speaking out against the project. “I chose the by the property out there and one day build a house because of the rural lifestyle. And I think you know when you have six data centers within a half mile radius, it’s really going to infringe on that,” said Sarcoxie resident Rhys Hine. But complaints about the potential impact aren’t only about electric use. “Like the water use is a big deal. Um, you know, we’re all in wells out there, so the water table will deplete. Um, the noise pollution, the light pollution,” said Hine. An online petition opposing the project has gotten nearly 1,900 signatures, but Jasper County does not have any independent regulatory authority. Meanwhile, a representative of Geronimo Power said by phone that they are working to be transparent and want to be a good neighbor.

By: Richmond BizSense Posted CHESTERFIELD COUNTY, Va. -- Google is booting up its highly touted plans to build data center facilities in Chesterfield County. The tech giant recently filed a site plan for the data center campus code-named Project Peanut, which is being planned on a 300-plus-acre site at 2700 Bermuda Hundred Road next to Meadowville Technology Park.

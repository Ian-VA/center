A proposal for an expansive data center campus in Bartow County is poised to become one of the area’s biggest projects — and largest electricity users. Preliminary plans for “Project Springbank” were disclosed Friday for a six-building data center campus an hour northwest of Atlanta, according to a Development of Regional Impact filing.

Third St. (Cushman & Wakefield via Cincinnati Business Courier) Comment on this story 1 Comment Share story Share topics: Data center Cincinnati DartPoints Expansion Artificial intelligence High-performance computing Investment Infrastructure CINCINNATI ( Cincinnati Business Courier ) - A national provider of digital infrastructure optimized for artificial intelligence and high-performance computing needs will expand and upgrade its downtown Cincinnati data center with next-generation technologies. Dallas-based DartPoints around 15 years ago created a data center in around 15,000 square feet of space, across two floors, in the nine-story building at 302 W. Third St. Two floors above the data center, it has a 15,000-square-foot office, staffed to provide 24/7 service to the critical power and cooling infrastructure. There are also some on-site marketing roles. Read the full story from the Cincinnati Business Courier.

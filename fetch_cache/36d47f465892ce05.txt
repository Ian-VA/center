Facebook Twitter WhatsApp SMS Email Print Copy article link Save CyrusOne wants to place another data center campus in Yorkville, this one on the east side of Beecher Road. The site would cover about 128 acres and consist of three data center buildings totaling 740,000 square feet, an electrical substation, and a detention basin for water. Project documents say the plan is connected in part with CyrusOne’s other approved data center project, located just to the west. The company is requesting annexation into the city, rezoning from agricultural to manufacturing, and approval of site plans. Hearings are scheduled for Tuesday and Wednesday, January 13 and 14, at 7 p.m. at Yorkville City Hall. A site plan provided by CyrusOne for the company newly proposed data center.

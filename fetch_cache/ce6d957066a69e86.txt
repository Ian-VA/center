Groundbreaking ceremony for Cedar Rapids data center cancelled Skip to content Share Add Us On Google Add as a preferred source on Google CEDAR RAPIDS, Iowa (KCRG) - A groundbreaking ceremony for a Cedar Rapids data center has been cancelled Tuesday. Senior Public Relations Manager Karen Cohen confirmed the event is cancelled following a shooting in New York Monday. The shooting took place at a Manhattan office tower housing Blackstone, the company that owns QTS. State and local leaders, including Governor Kim Reynolds, were expected to attend the event Tuesday. QTS co-CEOs also planned to attend the ceremony. Cohen shared a statement with KCRG-TV9 Monday night, stating further updates will be provided when available. The QTS facility is one of two data centers coming to the Big Cedar Industrial Center. Construction is already underway for Google’s center near the airport. Copyright 2025 KCRG. All rights reserved.

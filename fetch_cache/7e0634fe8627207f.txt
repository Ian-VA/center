Development Projects & Public Hearings Weather Development Projects & Public Hearings Click the drop-down menus below for information about planning and zoning related public hearings, development projects, and other items that require Planning Commission, Zoning Board of Appeals, or City Council review. Larger development projects require review and approval by the Planning Commission. Regulated uses (also called special uses), rezonings, and changes to the City’s Zoning Ordinance are reviewed by the Planning Commission and City Council. In these cases, the Planning Commission reviews the proposal and makes a recommendation to City Council for approval or denial. The Zoning Board of Appeals reviews variance requests, Zoning Ordinance interpretations, and appeals of administrative decisions. Public hearings at a Planning Commission, Zoning Board of Appeals, or City Council meeting may be one of several steps in the review process. Many planning and zoning projects take multiple meetings to review. Proposals are also frequently revised during the review process. Please see the agenda and minutes pages for the Planning Commission , Zoning Board of Appeals , or City Council for the most up to date application and meeting information, or contact the Building Department if you have questions.

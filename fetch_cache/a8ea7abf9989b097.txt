Submissions | King George Technology Center Skip to content King George Technology Center Rezoning Submission King George Technology Center Rezoning Submission Progress Continues for State-of-the-Art Data Center Campus The King George Technology Center is a state-of-the-art data center campus designed to drive economic growth while integrating thoughtful infrastructure improvements. We officially kicked off our Application on May 17, 2022, with our first Rezoning Submission on September 26, 2022. King George Technology Center Timeline A Vision for Growth & Innovation The proposal includes up to 6.5 million square feet of data center development, built in two phases based on market demand. The project will feature strategic transportation enhancements, including turn lanes and potential traffic signals along Route 3, as well as extensive buffering and architectural design standards to minimize visual impact. Additionally, 18 acres will be dedicated to King George County for public use, and Green Energy Ventures has committed to financial contributions to support public safety and county resources.

Download Now Frequently Asked Questions Why choose Orangeburg for your data center needs? Orangeburg is located approximately 30 miles from Manhattan, offering access to one of the largest economic hubs in the world without the high real estate and operating costs associated with being directly in NYC. This proximity also provides low-latency connectivity to businesses in the city, making it ideal for companies that need fast access to financial, tech, or media hubs. Does DataBank's Orangeburg facility support HPC? Yes. DataBank's Orangeburg, NY data center facility was built according to our Universal Data Hall Design . This ensures that the facility has the power, cooling, and floor space needed to support HPC deployments.

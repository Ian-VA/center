This expansion responds to rising market demand for scalable, low-latency infrastructure to support AI, cloud, and edge computing workloads. The program involves enhancing the Detroit Metro and Des Moines facilities by adding 3 MW and 6 MW of new commercial power capacity, respectively, along with installing more than 1,000 miles of ultra-dense fiber across strategic U.S. routes. CEO Daniel Watts emphasized that the company is developing much more than expanded capacity; they are establishing a digital platform designed to support advancements in artificial intelligence , cloud services, and edge computing for the years ahead. Backed by Igneo Infrastructure Partners, US Signal aims to position itself as a cornerstone digital infrastructure provider meeting growing enterprise and hyperscaler demands nationwide.

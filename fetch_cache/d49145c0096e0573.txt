AI data center by Digital Power will not open in Wisconsin Rapids MONEY $200 million AI data center no longer plans to open in Wisconsin Rapids. What we know. In January, Digital Power Optimization Inc. announced plans to build an AI data center in Wisconsin Rapids. They told the city in July they would not be moving forward with the project. Caitlin Shuda Wisconsin Rapids Daily Tribune July 23, 2025, 2:08 p.m. CT WISCONSIN RAPIDS − A multimillion dollar computing facility planned for Wisconsin Rapids is no longer happening. Digital Power Optimization Inc., a privately-held New York-based company, announced in January it planned to lease a 6-acre parcel of land from a 15-acre parcel owned by Consolidated Water Power Company and would develop a $200 million facility capable of up to 20 megawatts of artificial intelligence computing.

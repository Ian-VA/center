Aligned completes first Chicago data center, breaks ground on second - DCD Home News Construction & Site Selection Aligned completes first Chicago data center, breaks ground on second Company jumps straight into phase 2 of 100MW campus April 20, 2022 By Dan Swinhoe Have your say Aligned has completed work on the first data center at its Chicagoland campus, and broken ground on a new adjacent data center. The company this week announced first-phase completion of its ORD-01 facility in located in Northlake, Illinois; the facility offers 220,000 square feet of space and 48MW of capacity (expandable to 60 MW). – Aligned The company is simultaneously starting work on a second, adjacent facility, ORD-02. Upon completion, Aligned’s 18.5-acre Chicagoland campus will offer two multi-story facilities spanning 520,000 sq ft and offering more than 100MW of capacity. “Chicago’s centralized location, long-haul connectivity, and access to renewable energy options make it an ideal geostrategic destination for hyperscalers and multinational enterprises to deploy their mission-critical infrastructure,” said Andrew Schaap, CEO of Aligned. “Bringing one hyperscale data center online the same day that we break ground on a second facility is a testament not only to the demand for Aligned’s adaptive and sustainable data center platform, but to our ability to deliver infrastructure at the velocity our customers need it, even in a supply-and power-constrained market such as Chicago, anywhere in the world.” 2022 has seen Aligned complete work on a build-to-suit facility in Utah , and begin work on a new data center in Phoenix, Arizona.

LUTHER, Okla. - Monday evening, residents in Luther met to oppose a proposed data center in the area and the transmission line it would connect to. The proposed data center would cover about 400 acres between North Triple X Road and North Luther Road. The transmission line would be 40 miles, running from Luther to Piedmont. Meeting attendees shared multiple concerns with these proposed projects in the area. The main concerns in the area are that the data center will use Residents say they are worried that the projects will use excess water and that chemicals from the project could pollute water sources in the area. They are also concerned about who will be responsible for funding the bill. Some worry that these project coils make Luther feel industrial, from the noise and light pollution from the data center that would run 24/7. At this time, the plans for the data center have not been filed. The transmission line is expected to be completed in 2027. Destini Pittman Destini Pittman is a digital content producer at News 9.

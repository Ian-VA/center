It is adjacent to Ashburn Technology Park and extremely close to Loudon County Parkway, Dulles Toll Road, and Dulles International Airport. The 18-acre campus is home to IAD1, IAD3 and IAD4 data centers with rich campus cross-connect and interconnection options. The site is served by multiple feeds from an off-site, utility-owned substation providing 70MW of power. Both facilities on this campus are eligible for the State of Virginia Data Center Retail Sales & Use Tax exemption. DataBank’s Ashburn Campus is the ideal location in Northern Virginia for enterprise, web-scale SaaS or hyperscale cloud workloads that need room to expand and interconnect.

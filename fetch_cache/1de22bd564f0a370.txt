Georgia-based film studio considers converting to data center - DCD Home News The Data Center Construction Channel Georgia-based film studio considers converting to data center Downturn in Georgia film industry has led to an oversupply of space December 18, 2025 By Barney Dixon Have your say Athens, Georgia-based Athena Studios is considering converting into a data center, its CEO Joel Harber has said. – Sebastian Moss As reported by WSB-TV , the film studio has seen a significant decline in movie production demand, according to Harber, which has led to an “oversupply of space and an undersupply of demand.” Athens is a consolidated city-county about 70 miles (110 km) northeast of downtown Atlanta. Athena Studios’ premises spans 185,000 sq ft (17,187 sqm) and possesses adequate power and air conditioning. Film production spend in Georgia has dropped significantly. According to the Georgia Film Office, 245 film and television productions spent $2.3 billion as of June 2025, down by nearly half from the same period in 2022. The wider global film industry has also faced a downturn in recent years. Harber said he was hopeful that the studio would continue to make films, but would look at other alternatives and plan for the worst.

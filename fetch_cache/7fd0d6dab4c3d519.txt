TeraWulf and Fluidstack Secure Financing for 168 MW Abernathy HPC Project, Setting Stage for AI Data Center Growth Successful Financing Paves Way for High-Capacity, Liquid-Cooled AI Data Center TeraWulf Inc. (NASDAQ:WULF) and Fluidstack have finalized project-level financing for their joint 168 megawatt (MW) high-performance computing (HPC) data center venture in Abernathy, Texas. The secured funding backs the construction of a next-generation, liquid-cooled facility designed to deliver up to 240 MW of total power, with 168 MW dedicated as critical IT load, under a long-term hosting agreement featuring investment-grade credit support. Strategic Advantages: Long-Term Support and Scalability The Abernathy project’s credit profile receives a boost from Fluidstack’s platform, supported by a leading hyperscale partner. The resulting financial structure allows TeraWulf and Fluidstack to proceed confidently with facility buildout, targeted for completion and commissioning in the second half of 2026. The partnership has established the framework for potential expansion beyond the initial 168 MW, thanks to pre-secured land, transmission, and infrastructure at the site. Growth Drivers: Rising HPC Demand and Flexible Expansion Plans As demand for AI compute capacity grows, the Abernathy project’s scale and design place it in a competitive position. Fluidstack’s platform connects a broad and expanding customer base with the project’s combination of time-to-market, total cost, reliability, and advanced execution. Importantly, TeraWulf and Fluidstack have set themselves up for future scalability—retaining the ability to rapidly deploy additional high-density capacity as market demand accelerates. Market Momentum: TeraWulf Targets Significant Expansion Pipeline The Abernathy financing builds on TeraWulf and Fluidstack’s reputation as early movers in large-scale AI infrastructure development and project financing. In a market where available capacity is tightening, TeraWulf projects the ability to add 250 MW to 500 MW of HPC capacity annually and has already locked in over 500 MW for future deployments. This significant pipeline offers flexibility to meet customer demand as the AI data center market expands.

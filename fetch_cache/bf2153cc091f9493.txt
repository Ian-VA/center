Microsoft changed its data center plans because of these issues BUSINESS Inside the opposition that led Microsoft to change course on its Caledonia data center plan Secrecy, lack of details were factors Ricardo Torres Milwaukee Journal Sentinel Oct. 9, 2025 Updated Oct. 10, 2025, 1:18 p.m. CT Data centers opponents often cite the facilities' heavy water and energy use, but Caledonia residents have other concerns — including a process that seemed secretive and lacking in details. After residents and elected officials voiced their issues, Microsoft Corp. abandoned its plans to have 244 acres of farm land rezoned for a data center.

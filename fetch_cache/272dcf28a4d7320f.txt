Vantage files plans for San Antonio data center - DCD Home News Construction & Site Selection Vantage files plans for San Antonio data center To be located in Texas Research Park December 19, 2023 By Georgia Butler Have your say Plans for a data center in San Antonio, Texas, have been filed with the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR). The location provided - 14720 Omicron Drive, San Antonio, TX 78245 - sits within land purchased by Vantage Data Centers in July 2023 from the University of Texas System Board of Regents. – Google Maps The TDLR filing suggests that work on the data center, dubbed TX11, began on December 15, with an estimated completion date of January 2026. The data center, which will span 242,667 sq ft (22,545 sqm) of floorspace, will be a two-story building with a total capacity of 32MW across four data halls, each with 8MW. The filing estimates that the project will cost close to $96 million. In addition to the Omicron Drive development, Vantage purchased 31 acres on the north corner of State Highway 151 and Wiseman Boulevard, also in San Antonio, in August 2023 . Both land parcels are near a large Microsoft campus at 15000 Lambda Drive, where the company recently completed another 79,385 sq ft (7,375 sqm) data center, known as SAT46 . CloudHQ and QTS are also looking to develop in the Texas Research Park, while Amazon is planning three data centers in the San Antonio area.

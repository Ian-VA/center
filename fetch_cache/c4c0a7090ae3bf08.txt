Vantage eyes Millersport, Ohio for new data center campus NEWS Central Ohio in line for $1 billion-plus data center. Here's what we know Max Filby Columbus Dispatch Updated May 30, 2025, 9:32 a.m. ET A more than $1 billion data center campus planned for Millersport is expected to eventually create hundreds of new jobs. Construction is expected to begin before the end of the year, and the first building on the data center campus could open in 2027, Fairfield County Economic Development Director Rick Szabrak told The Dispatch.

Infrakey, City of Lacy Lakeview unveil 925MW data center district in McLennan County, Texas - DCD Home News The Data Center Construction Channel Infrakey, City of Lacy Lakeview unveil 925MW data center district in McLennan County, Texas The site covers 520 acres and will be developed in phases January 21, 2026 By Zachary Skidmore Have your say Infrakey DC Parks and the City of Lacy Lakeview in Texas have unveiled the Lacy Lakeview Data District (L2D2). The public-private partnership covers around 520 acres in McLennan County, Texas, with a planned power capacity of up to 925MW. – Google Maps According to the partners, the park is designed to provide an infrastructure framework that will support the development of hyperscale, AI-driven data centers. Previous reports have suggested that, at full build-out, the site could host up to six data centers with 1GW of capacity. The agreement, formalized during the Pacific Telecommunications Council 2026 Conference in Honolulu, aims to integrate Infrakey-controlled land into the City of Lacy Lakeview’s jurisdiction to establish a dedicated regulatory and infrastructure pathway to accelerate data center deployments. Instead of a single-tenant campus, L2D2 is expected to be structured as a district-scale infrastructure platform. The partners claim that the project will offer a coordinated stack that includes ERCOT grid interconnection with a district substation, behind-the-meter power generation and energy storage, microgrid controls and energy optimization, reclaimed water sourced through the City’s wastewater system, multi-path fiber connectivity, and optional district cooling. "Hyperscale and AI customers no longer want speculative builds; they want infrastructure certainty," said Braham Singh, CEO of Infrakey DC Parks. "The Data District model is designed to provide a coordinated, infrastructure-ready environment that supports faster deployment and long-term operational stability for our tenants." Phase I of the project is expected to deploy 300MW of grid-based power, with 150MW available through an initial substation interconnection, and an additional 150MW of capacity slated for connection by the end of 2027. The partners claim that at full build-out, the project will comprise more than $2 billion in infrastructure investment, with the district designed to scale over several years. Infrakey DC Parks is a subsidiary of Infrakey Capital, a global investment firm that focuses on the development and financing of large-scale digital infrastructure parks. According to the company’s website , it has five data center parks in development, across four countries, with a planned capacity of up to 5GW.

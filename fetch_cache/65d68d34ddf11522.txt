Outcry grows over proposed data center campus | 28/22 News Skip to content by: Brian Orbin Posted: Nov 10, 2025 / 11:33 PM EST Updated: Nov 12, 2025 / 09:44 AM EST SHARE HAZLE TOWNSHIP, LUZERNE COUNTY (WBRE/WYOU) — A proposed data center in Hazle Township has neighbors fired up, worried about everything from constant noise to rising utility costs. It was standing room only inside the Hazle Township meeting room, a crowd packed with frustration and fear. Residents living near the proposed NorthPoint data center say the peace and quiet of their Eagle Rock neighborhood has already been disrupted. “I’m upset, and I’m appalled that you would even consider this. Because everyone knows, if you say yes, the whole area is going to be done,” Hazle Township resident Doreen Cherenyock explained. Neighbors describe bright lights, grinding, and banging through the night. Even though the township ordinance bans overnight operations between 10:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. “We’re very concerned that once it comes there, it’ll create this constant humming noise, which everyone in Eagle Rock will hear anyway, and there’s no way to pull it back after that,” Alexander Sloot from Sugarloaf said. More than a dozen buildings are planned across roughly 1,300 acres off State Route 924. Some worry that it will drain resources and threaten health and safety. “We’ve seen communities in northern Virginia regret approving similar projects, only to face water shortages, bridge strain, and rising utility costs. They also cause increased cancer risk and noise pollution. It’s a loud humming like a generator that never stops. Will it affect the pillars holding up the mines?” Sherri Homancos from Hazle Township explained. The public pushback then led to a pause. “I think we need time to look into this and digest it. So I’d like to make a motion to table this,” Hazle Township Board of Supervisors Vice Chairman Bob Fiume said. Others say the township failed to fully explain what’s at stake, including a possible tie to a 12-mile 500 kilovolt PPL utility line project in nearby Sugarloaf Township. “I don’t think that our town officials really made us aware of how this could possibly impact us, so we can voice our opinions,” Bob Zafian from Hazle Township said. An executive session followed the meeting to further discuss the future of the project. For now, every agenda item related to the NorthPoint data center is on hold. Township officials aren’t saying when the issue will return to the agenda.

Tier III AI data center proposed for Lewiston Bates Mill in Maine - DCD Home News The Data Center Construction Channel Tier III AI data center proposed for Lewiston Bates Mill in Maine The proposal is still waiting for approval by city councilors December 15, 2025 By Barney Dixon Have your say A new Tier III AI data center has been proposed in downtown Lewiston, Maine, in the currently vacant Bates Mill No.3. As reported by WGME , the proposed project, which is being developed by MillCompute LLC, would become the state’s second-largest AI data center. Lewiston City administrator Bryan Kaenrath said the project would bring in just under $800,000 in tax revenue for the city, as well as create 20-30 jobs in the area. The proposed data center will take up the first two floors of Bates Mill No.3, around 85,000 sq ft (7,896 sqm). Vocal opposition to the proposed data center has already started, with the Facebook group “ Mainers against future AI data center in Lewiston ” already garnering more than 100 members. A memo sent to Lewiston city councilors said MillCompute’s team includes “local leadership and experts from across the country in data center construction, operation, and finance.” City councilors still need to decide on whether or not to approve the project at the council’s next regular meeting on December 16.

Developer plans $33 billion data center in Pinal County PINAL $33 billion data center development planned for Pinal County Corina Vanek Arizona Republic July 30, 2025, 5:01 a.m. MT One of Arizona’s largest private landowners has plans to develop the state’s largest data center project, a development estimated to cost $33 billion. Kuldip Verma, CEO of Vermaland, is priming a 3,300-acre site near Eloy for the development, which could support up to three gigawatts of power capacity. The project would bring more capacity than the Valley currently has in existing data center space.

Vantage files to build another data center in San Antonio, Texas - DCD Home News The Data Center Construction Channel Vantage files to build another data center in San Antonio, Texas Company files for second building at second campus in city October 15, 2025 By Dan Swinhoe Have your say Data center operator Vantage has filed to develop another facility in San Antonio, Texas. Vantage has filed a new Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) application with the state of Texas for a data center. – Google Maps TX22, located at 5207 Rogers Road in Bexar County, is described as a 214,525 sq ft (19,930 sqm), two-story data center. Construction on the $272 million project is set to run from October 2025 until June 2027. Corgan is listed at the project’s design firm. Vantage purchased 31 acres on the north corner of State Highway 151 and Wiseman Boulevard in August 2023 . It filed to develop TX21, a 360,000 sq ft (33,445 sqm), three-story data center at the site on 5207 Rogers Road in November 2023. Construction on the $157m project was set to start in December 2023 for a June 2025 launch date. It's unclear if that facility has launched yet. Last month, Vantage filed to develop another facility at its other campus in the city. The TX12 project, to be located at 17420 Omicron Drive in Bexar County, will be a two-story building with up to 96MW of critical IT capacity across 432,800 sq ft (40,208 sqm). The $276.8m project is set to run from October 2025 until August 2027. The company acquired land along Omicron Drive in July 2023 from the University of Texas System Board of Regents. Vantage filed for another data center at the campus – TX11 – in December 2023 . That project – a 32MW two-story building spanning 242,665 sq ft (22,545 sqm) – is set to go live in early 2026. Vantage is yet to make any announcements about its projects in San Antonio. Both campuses are near a large Microsoft campus at 15000 Lambda Drive, where the company recently completed another 79,385 sq ft (7,375 sqm) data center, known as SAT46 . CloudHQ , CyrusOne , and QTS are also looking to develop in the Texas Research Park, while Amazon is planning three data centers in the San Antonio area. Elsewhere in Texas, Vantage is developing a large campus outside Abilene in Shackelford County. The 1.4GW Frontier Campus will space 1,200 acres and host some 10 data centers totaling 3.7 million sq ft (343,741 sqm). Construction on the campus has already begun, with the first building scheduled for delivery in the second half of 2026.

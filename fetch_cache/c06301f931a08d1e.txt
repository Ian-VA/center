Data center proposed on 580 acres in New Castle County, Delaware BUSINESS Massive 11-building data center proposed near Delaware City, bringing hundreds of jobs Ben Mace Delaware News Journal July 17, 2025, 5:37 a.m. ET A data center with 11 buildings totaling about 6 million square feet has been proposed northwest of Delaware City with projections of creating hundreds of jobs. New Castle County has received a plan from property owner New Castle Campus Development LLC and developer Starwood Digital Ventures for the project on about 580 acres on two properties.

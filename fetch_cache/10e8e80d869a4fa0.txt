records. File Photo | The Lima News LIMA — Properties in American and Sugar Creek townships at the center of a proposed data center have now officially changed hands, with the company behind the as-yet-unnamed technology corporation behind the proposal now owning three parcels in the vicinity of North Cole Street between Irvin and Beery Roads and Bluelick Road. According to records from the Allen County Recorder’s website, Bistrozzi LLC, the Wilmington, Delaware-based front company for this project, completed its purchases of three parcels in American and Sugar Creek townships last week. Bistrozzi purchased the property at 950 W. Bluelick Road from the Kyle C. Brenneman Living Trust, Kyle Brenneman, Sarah Brenneman, Keaton Brenneman and Kristen Brenneman for $3,564,000, along with property along Bluelick Road from Pike Run Farms LLC for $2,347,000 and just over 200 acres at 4110 N. Cole St. from Neff Farms, Inc. for $8,524,300. This brings the total purchase price for all three properties to $14,435,300. This purchase represents only a portion of what the company behind the data center is prepared to invest, according to Allen Economic Development Group President and CEO Cynthia Leis. Along with the estimated $14.5 million in anticipated construction costs, the company has already secured agreements with Elida schools, paying the district $250,000 every year for 15 years in a tax abatement offset, as well as with the Allen County Sanitary Engineer, the City of Lima and the Allen County Board of Commissioners for wastewater, water supply and roadway improvements, respectively. “Between all three of those agreements, it’s approximately $50 million of infrastructure improvements,” she said in a July 31 interview. “They’re going to cover 100 percent on the infrastructure improvements needed for them to start construction and be operational.” No site work will begin until the crops currently growing on those properties have been harvested, Leis said, with the company mandated to begin work no later than February 2026.
